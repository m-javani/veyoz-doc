About this project

Why Veyoz ended, and what it left behind.

Veyoz is a search-and-pricing engine for OTA inventory, built in Rust between late 2024 and mid-2025. It reached a working state: reproducible benchmarks, a passing end-to-end test suite, a documented wire format, and complete public documentation.

Development was discontinued mid-2025. The reason was not technical. The companies Veyoz was built for — travel platforms — became unable to operate in or with Iran due to the political situation that escalated that year. Without a reachable market, further development had no path forward.

The documentation, benchmarks, and binary are left public for reference. The source is not open. Anyone interested in the underlying design is welcome to reach out.

— Mehdi Javani, September 2025
